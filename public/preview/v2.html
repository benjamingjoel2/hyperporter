    FAQ

    Your questionsanswered

    Do we have to change our existing systems?+
    No. Autopilot connects to the channels inquiries already arrive on. Nothing migrates and your clients see no change in how they reach you.
    Can I use Autopilot with my own operators?+
    Yes — that is one of the three ways to buy. Subscribe to Autopilot and run it entirely on the partners you already work with. Horizon is optional and sold separately.
    Can I use Horizon without the software?+
    Yes. Bring us the trip directly and we will run it through the network for you. No subscription required.
    Does the operator see my margin?+
    No. Your markup is applied before anything reaches the client, and the operator never sees what you charged. Neither side has the other's number.
    Is payment automated?+
    Deliberately not. Every settlement is marked by a person. Money is the one part of the chain we do not let an automation touch.
    Can we start with one destination?+
    Yes, and most agencies do. Run one region through Autopilot first, then widen once your coordinators trust what the board is telling them.
