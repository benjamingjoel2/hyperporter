Proposal Share overview

Built, not written

The proposal draws its days, its prices and its terms from the trip record. Change the itinerary and the proposal changes with it.

A link that stays current

The traveller’s magic link always shows the latest version. There is no v3 in one inbox and v4 in another.

Your brand on it

Your logo, your colours, your name. From Autopilot up the Hyperporter badge comes off; on Intelligence the link is on your own domain.

How sharing works

One action turns the chosen quotes and the itinerary into a proposal page.

Send the link, export a PDF, or text the link — whichever the traveller will actually open.

The trip record shows when the link was opened and how many times, so a follow-up is timed rather than guessed.

The traveller accepts on their side of the link. The trip moves to Confirmation and the supplier is re-checked.
